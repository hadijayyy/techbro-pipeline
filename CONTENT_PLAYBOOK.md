### Topik Prioritas (scoring boost + editorial priority)

### Hook Formula Terbukti
- **Angka spesifik + kontras absurd + dampak dompet langsung**
  ✅ "Gaji UMR Jakarta cuma Rp5jt/bulan, tapi biaya HARIAN di Greenland US$1.171 (Rp21jt)"
  ✅ "Rp80 TRILIUN bisa dihemat dari Program Makan Bergizi Gratis 2026"
  ❌ "Jujur, gue KAGET soal..." (hanya work kalau relatable Indo)

### CTA Pattern Terbaik
- **Binary question** = debat = replies > 20
- **"Cerita di komen"** = shared experience = organic replies
- **Open polemic** = "Menurut lo...?" = diverse opinions
